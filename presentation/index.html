Part 2: Academic & Architectural Research Gap

Critical Industry Failures & The Blynt Gap

Analyzing the fundamental flaws in existing fintech tools and how Blynt bridges the gap between academic accounting rigor and modern SaaS.

🔬
1. Floating-Point Drift
Legacy web apps utilize standard IEEE-754 binary floats (e.g., 0.1 + 0.2 = 0.30000000000000004). Over hundreds of thousands of transactions, rounding drift corrupts ledger equality.
Blynt: Fixed-point NUMERIC(19,4) Decimal
⚠️
2. Unchecked Financial AI
LLMs are probabilistic, but double-entry accounting is strictly deterministic. Off-the-shelf OCR engines frequently flip sales into purchases or hallucinate non-existent tax amounts.
Blynt: 15-Rule Validation Guardrails
🛡️
3. Vulnerable Audit Trails
Most SaaS audit logs live in standard mutable tables where application bugs or malicious actors can update or delete audit records without detection.
Blynt: Database Append-Only Triggers
